# The lab notebook

The notebook is a two-tab book of what you have learned. Open it with **Notebook** on the campaign header (the button reads **Notes** on a phone-sized window). It has two tabs, **Notes** and **Concepts**. A **What is real** button on its header opens [that page](what-is-real.html). The page scrolls with the mouse wheel, by touch drag, or with the D-pad and stick.

## Notes: one cited finding per level

Each level has a notebook entry. It is a short, sourced fact about the real nervous system that relates to the level. It unlocks the first time you pass the level, in any mode. A diagnosis case's entry unlocks when you solve it. A level that has no entry yet is simply absent from the book.

An entry has a title, a paragraph and a citation. For example, the first level, Threshold, has an entry titled "Rheobase" about Louis Lapicque's 1907 measurements. The entries say where the game differs from the animal. The touch-reflex entry, for instance, says that real touch receptors are mostly silent at rest, whereas the game's fire at 150 Hz so that there is noise to reject.

The notes are grouped by chapter in play order. A card for a level you have not passed is a pale ruled card that says "Not written yet". Under it, if the level is locked, it says what to pass first ("Pass X first to unlock."); if the level is open it says "Pass this level to unlock." The header shows "n of N entries unlocked".

Every reference is in the game's citation registry (`docs/CITATIONS.md` in the repository). Most were read from the PubMed record or checked against Crossref by DOI. The ones that could not be (older books and papers that PubMed does not index, roughly a fifth of the list) are marked "unverified" there. See [contributing](contributing.html).

## Concepts: the glossary inside the game

Every level introduces at most one new idea. The Concepts tab collects them. Each concept has a title, a one-paragraph definition, a citation, the level that introduces it, and the later levels that use it again.

A concept you have not met shows as "???" with "Learned by passing <level>". When you pass that level the card fills in, and it says "Introduced in <level>" and lists the later levels you have passed that use it again. The header shows "n of N concepts learned". A concept is learned by passing the level that introduces it, in any mode.

There are 45 concepts, from **Threshold and rheobase** in the first level to **Population vector** in the last Theory chapter. They are listed one line each in [the glossary](glossary.html).

## Concept chips on the map

On the campaign map, the level card in the side panel shows its concepts as chips: **NEW IDEA** (what it introduces), **USES** (what it uses from earlier levels) and **NEEDS** (uses that you have not met yet, each with the level that teaches it). Needs is information only, except that a few Bestiary chapters wait for a particular level; see [the body chapters](campaign-body.html). The "Know first" column of the campaign pages is the USES list: [body chapters](campaign-body.html), [Bestiary](campaign-bestiary.html), [Theory](campaign-theory.html).
