# The lab notebook

The notebook is a two-tab book of what you have learned. Open it with **Notebook** on the campaign header (**Notes** on a phone-sized window). Its tabs are **Notes** and **Concepts**. The **What is real** button on its header opens [that page](what-is-real.html). Scroll with the mouse wheel, a touch drag, or the D-pad and stick.

## Notes: one cited finding per level

Each level has a notebook entry: a short, sourced fact about the real nervous system. It unlocks the first time you pass the level, in any mode. A diagnosis case's entry unlocks when you solve it. A level with no entry is absent from the book.

An entry has a title, a paragraph and a citation. The first level, Threshold, has an entry titled "Rheobase" about Louis Lapicque's 1907 measurements. Entries say where the game differs from the animal. The touch-reflex entry says real touch receptors are mostly silent at rest, whereas the game's fire at 150 Hz so that there is noise to reject.

Notes are grouped by chapter in play order. A level you have not passed gets a pale ruled card that says "Not written yet". Under it, a locked level says what to pass first ("Pass X first to unlock."). An open level says "Pass this level to unlock." The header shows "n of N entries unlocked".

Every reference is in the game's citation registry (`docs/CITATIONS.md` in the repository). Most were read from the PubMed record or checked against Crossref by DOI. The rest (older books and papers that PubMed does not index, roughly a fifth of the list) are marked "unverified" there. See [contributing](contributing.html).

## Concepts: the glossary inside the game

Every level introduces at most one new idea, and the Concepts tab collects them. Each concept has a title, a one-paragraph definition, a citation, the level that introduces it, and the later levels that reuse it.

A concept you have not met shows as "???" with "Learned by passing <level>". Pass that level, in any mode, and the card fills in. It says "Introduced in <level>" and lists the later levels you have passed that reuse it. The header shows "n of N concepts learned".

There are 45 concepts, from **Threshold and rheobase** in the first level to **Population vector** in the last Theory chapter. [The glossary](glossary.html) lists them one line each.

## Concept chips on the map

On the campaign map, the level card in the side panel shows its concepts as chips: **NEW IDEA** (what it introduces), **USES** (what it uses from earlier levels) and **NEEDS** (uses you have not met yet, each with the level that teaches it). Needs is information only, except that a few Bestiary chapters wait for a particular level; see [the body chapters](campaign-body.html). The "Know first" column of the campaign pages is the USES list: [body chapters](campaign-body.html), [Bestiary](campaign-bestiary.html), [Theory](campaign-theory.html).
