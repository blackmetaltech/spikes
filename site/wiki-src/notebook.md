# The lab notebook

The notebook is a two-tab book of what you have learned. Open it with **Notebook** on the campaign header. It has two tabs, **Notes** and **Concepts**. A **What is real** button on its header opens [that page](what-is-real.html).

## Notes: one cited finding per level

Each level has a notebook entry. It is a short, sourced fact about the real nervous system that relates to the level. It unlocks the first time you pass the level. A diagnosis case's entry unlocks when you solve it.

An entry has a title, a paragraph and a citation. For example, the first level, Threshold, has an entry titled "Rheobase" about Louis Lapicque's 1907 measurements. The entries say where the game differs from the animal. The touch-reflex entry, for instance, says that real touch receptors are mostly silent at rest, whereas the game's fire at 150 Hz so that there is noise to reject.

The notes are grouped by chapter in play order. A card for a level you have not passed is a pale ruled card that says "Not written yet" and tells you what to pass first. The header shows "n of N entries unlocked".

Every reference is in the game's citation registry and was checked against PubMed or Crossref with a DOI. A few older books and papers could not be verified and are marked as such in the repository's citation list. See [contributing](contributing.html).

## Concepts: the glossary inside the game

Every level introduces at most one new idea. The Concepts tab collects them. Each concept has a title, a one-paragraph definition, a citation, the level that introduces it, and the later levels that use it again.

A concept you have not met shows as "???" with "Learned by passing <level>". When you pass that level the card fills in. The header shows "n of N concepts learned".

There are 45 concepts, from **Threshold and rheobase** in the first level to **Population vector** in the last Theory chapter. They are listed one line each in [the glossary](glossary.html).

## Concept chips on the map

On the campaign map a level's card shows its concepts as chips: what it introduces and what it uses from earlier levels. The "Know first" column of the campaign pages follows the same data: [body chapters](campaign-body.html), [Bestiary](campaign-bestiary.html), [Theory](campaign-theory.html).
