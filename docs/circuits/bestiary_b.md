# Bestiary chapters B: fly compass, fly mushroom body, crab

Fifteen levels (`engine/src/tasks/{c_ring,c_shift,fly_compass,dx_compass,k_sparse,k_depress,fly_mb,dx_mb_apl,dx_mb_dan,s_pacemaker,s_phase,crab_pyloric,dx_crab}.cpp`, shared scaffolding `bestiary_a.h` and `bestiary_b.h`). Reference circuits: `circuits/*.json`, generated by `circuits/tools/gen_bestiary_b.py`. Research: `research/bestiary-a.md` sections 4 to 6; honest limits: `docs/WHAT-IS-REAL.md`. Same contract as the chapters A (`docs/circuits/bestiary_a.md`): a scripted world, a mechanism check on `Engine::structure()`, verdicts and hint tiers 1 to 3 without a digit, a recipe, a cited notebook entry, a quick episode, cheap circuits that must fail (`engine/tests/cheap_rows_e.inc`, `tasks_cheap_test_e`), levels and episodes in `engine/tests/tasks_bestiary_b_test.cpp`.

| Level | Teaches | Mechanism the check demands | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| `c_ring` | ring attractor | `heading` read as a population vector over the ring; excitatory loop on the ring; net-inhibitory loop (Delta7); every landmark sensor wired | 20 cells |
| `c_shift` | path integration | the above, plus two separate shifter groups, each excited by the ring and exciting it, driven by `turn_left` and `turn_right` | 52 cells |
| `fly_compass` | heading in the dark | the same; scored on the heading error through the population vector after holding still and after four turns | 52 cells |
| `k_sparse` | sparse coding | glomeruli > PN > KC with at most 8 inputs per KC; KC > APL > KC net inhibition; 30 or more KCs; the reference has 200 KCs (224 cells), par 250 | 224 cells |
| `k_depress` | dopamine-gated depression | a `stdp_dopamine_synapse` from the odour cells to the avoidance cells whose dopamine source is driven by the punishment sensor | 18 cells |
| `fly_mb` | the whole learning loop | both of the above; KC reaches the MBON; odour B is spared | 231 cells |
| `s_pacemaker` | pacemaker kernel | AB and PD are conductance-based cells (not integrate-and-fire) and coupled both ways | 3 cells |
| `s_phase` | phase by rebound | `pd` reaches the follower by inhibition; the follower is conductance-based | 1 cell |
| `crab_pyloric` | the pyloric rhythm | three separate read-outs, pacemaker inhibits LP and PY, LP inhibits PY, conductance-based cells | 6 cells |

Diagnosis: `dx_compass` (PENL > EPG cut), `dx_mb_apl` (APL > KC cut), `dx_mb_dan` (TSN > DAN cut), `dx_crab` (LP > PY cut, on the second parameter set); patient circuits are the references with the answer's weights at 0.

## Choices

**Compass.** 16 wedges (the research sheet allows 8 to 16): with 8 the discrete ring hopped unevenly and tracking errors of 90 degrees were common; with 16 the bump moves smoothly. The turn signal is **dc**, not Poisson: Poisson turn input made the gain vary by 30 degrees from seed to seed. The landmark is one of 16 sensors `cue0 ... cue15` (one per ring cell, chosen by the seed), so that the player wires a numbered family once and a circuit that holds only one bearing fails. The ring cells need a slow excitatory synapse (`tau_syn_ex` 15 ms) and a bias just under rheobase: with fast synapses and no bias the 16-cell spiking ring did not hold a bump. Gain: the reference follows turns of 120 to 170 degrees per second to within about 50 degrees; below about 60 degrees per second the bump does not move (a dead zone of the discrete ring), and above about 225 it breaks up, so the world uses 120 to 170.

**Dopamine-gated learning.** `stdp_dopamine_synapse` is enough: its eligibility trace is set by pre and post spikes and the weight changes only while dopamine is present, so a silent KC is never changed. NEST subtracts `A_minus` for anti-causal pairs, so the depression needs `A_plus < 0` and **`A_minus > 0`** (both negative gave a trace of mixed sign and erratic results). The avoidance cells need baseline firing so that pairs exist. No NESTML synapse and no projection-modulation row was needed. The dopamine source is a group of spiking cells (DAN) driven by a relay (TSN) from the `punish` sensor. Add-only engine pieces: `ProjectionInfo::dopamine_source` (so the check can follow the dopamine source) and `Engine::output_spiked` (distinct active cells, for sparseness).

**Crab cell.** The full seven-current model (Prinz-style) was used (NESTML `models/nestml/stg_cell.nestml`); it was stable at 0.1 ms and 1 ms task steps and needed no reduced burster. The AB cell bursts at about 0.9 s with `g_KCa` 3140 nS. Gap junctions: the engine allows `gap_junction` only on `hh_psc_alpha_gap` and `hh_cond_beta_gap_traub`, which do not burst, so AB and PD are coupled by reciprocal excitation (100 nS and 20 nS). The followers' parameter sets and synapses were found by a random search over follower conductances and weights with a pass test on the pattern (3 of 360 draws passed); two were kept by hand (`crab_pyloric.json`, `crab_pyloric_b.json`; a third was dropped because it was bistable across seeds). The analysis window starts at 3 s: the first seconds hold a start-up transient (the engine draws a random initial V_m).
