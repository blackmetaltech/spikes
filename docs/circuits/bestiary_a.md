## leech_heart: escape or release (the rework)

**What the level measures.** After a 12 s baseline (the rhythm as built: alternation, overlap, balance, bursts, period) the task adds 5 % of the drive current to the cells whose activity goes with the left motor (found through the circuit structure: the cells that excite or are `left` and inhibit `right`, so the reference's `HN_R` and a circuit that reads the interneurons directly alike). It reads the median length of the left side's active phases (first to last spike of a burst) and silent phases (last spike to the next burst) over [3, 15] s and over [18, 30] s. `dA` and `dS` are the relative changes. Release: `dA >= 0.08` (guided about 0.05, researcher about 0.12) and `dA > -dS`; escape: `-dS >= 0.08` and `-dS > dA`; neither fails with "the phase that sets the pace belongs to a cell's own slow process". The rhythm must also keep alternating under the test current. Source of the idea: Skinner, Kopell & Marder 1994 (four mechanisms of a two-cell half-centre; the period of the intrinsic ones does not depend on the synaptic threshold, that of the synaptic ones does) and Sharp, Skinner & Marder 1996 (dynamic clamp: the synaptic threshold changes the period and the mechanism, and a hyperpolarisation-activated current was needed for stable oscillation). The game's own manipulation (extra current into one cell) is the cell-level version of those experiments; its thresholds are game values.

**Measured on the reference (`HN` b 5 pA, tau_w 1500 ms, -50 nS), 3 modes x 35 seeds (1-30, 101-105), quick = full:** all 105 runs pass; `dA` +19 to +26 %, `dS` -9 to -3 %, period 1775 to 2550 ms (researcher band 1060 to 2980), 8 to 9 turns in each window. The spread of the period over seeds, with the drive gain noise of the game (3 % standard, 6 % researcher), is 2000 to 2400 ms in standard mode and 1775 to 2550 ms in researcher mode. Other release pairs pass as well (b 10 pA, tau_w 500 ms: dA +86 %), and the pair read straight from the interneurons.

**Why no escape reference.** What was tried (raw runs of the same four-cell circuit, 2 s symmetry-breaking start as in the task; a pair counts as alternating with at least 5 bursts in 20 s):
- `aeif_cond_alpha` with sub-threshold adaptation only (a 10 to 60 nS, b 0, tau_w 400 to 2000 ms; leak reversal -70 mV): alternates only in a narrow window of drive (a 20 nS, tau_w 1000 ms: period 654, 2417 ms and no rhythm for drive x0.94, x1.0, x1.06) and reads as release (a 15 pA test current lengthens the driven side's active phase by 70 %, its silent phase by -13 %).
- The same cell with the leak reversal moved next to the threshold (E_L -52 mV, V_th -50 mV; drive 5 % of the usual) alternates robustly (period 1788, 1853, 1903 ms for drive x0.94, x1.0, x1.06) and also reads as release (dA +10 %, dS 0 %). Raising E_L above the threshold, to put the active cell's adaptation near zero, gave no alternation in the 6 combinations tried; random scans of 148 combinations of a, tau_w, E_L, V_reset, g_L, Delta_T, drive and inhibition found 8 alternating ones, all with dS between -5 % and +8 %.
- A depressing inhibitory synapse (`tsodyks2_synapse`) between non-adapting `iaf_cond_alpha` cells: the steady inhibition of a depressing synapse is limited by w / tau_rec whatever the presynaptic rate (500 nS, 800 ms: about 3 nS mean conductance), so the pair fires together in every setting tried (16 combinations of w, U, tau_rec and drive).
- A slow disinhibitory loop (a slowly charging cell inhibited by its interneuron and exciting it, standing in for a hyperpolarisation-activated current): 60 random settings gave only fast switching (cycles of a few hundred ms), no steady slow alternation.
- `stg_cell` pairs (g_H and g_CaT varied, 48 settings): one cell wins and the other stays silent. `sa_node_pacemaker` pairs beat single beats on their own (no bursts). `ht_neuron` has no inhibitory port in the circuit format.
A cell with a hyperpolarisation-activated current and a leak that does not itself fatigue the active side, in the parts bin, would make the escape kind buildable (a NESTML cell, `docs/models/`); that is the open item. The task's escape branch is untested on a circuit for that reason.
