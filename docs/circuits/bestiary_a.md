# Bestiary chapters A: worm, leech, sea slug

Twelve levels (`engine/src/tasks/{w_latch,w_switch,worm_touch,l_burst,l_rebound,leech_heart,a_depress,a_facilitate,aplysia_gill,dx_worm,dx_leech,dx_aplysia}.cpp`, shared scaffolding `bestiary_a.h`). Reference circuits: `circuits/*.json`, generated by `circuits/tools/gen_bestiary_a.py` (the worm task by `tools/connectome/worm.py`). Research: `docs/research/bestiary-a.md`; honest limits: `docs/WHAT-IS-REAL.md`.

Each level is a scripted world (input schedule, measurement windows, a judge), a mechanism check on `Engine::structure()` that names the missing part, numberless verdicts and hints, a recipe with the numbers, a cited notebook entry and a quick episode. Cheap circuits that must fail: `engine/tests/cheap_rows_d.inc` (`tasks_cheap_test_d`); levels, diagnosis cases and episodes: `engine/tests/tasks_bestiary_a_test.cpp`.

| Level | Teaches | Mechanism the check demands | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| `w_latch` | persistence | touch > excitatory loop > reverse; stop reaches it by inhibition | 3 cells |
| `w_switch` | command switch (winner takes all) | two motors on two cells, inhibition both ways | 2 cells |
| `worm_touch` | the touch circuit | head and tail pathways, a holding loop on each side, mutual inhibition | 17 cells from Cook 2019 counts |
| `l_burst` | bursting | the cell has a slow recovery variable (not plain integrate-and-fire) | 1 Izhikevich cell |
| `l_rebound` | post-inhibitory rebound | silenced by an inhibitory cell; target with strong sub-threshold adaptation | 2 cells |
| `leech_heart` | half-centre heartbeat | mutual inhibition between the sides, adapting inhibitors; period band (time compressed) | 4 cells |
| `a_depress` | short-term depression | a depressing synapse (tsodyks2) on the touch path | 2 cells |
| `a_facilitate` | heterosynaptic facilitation | shock > cell that releases a modulator > modulation row on the touch synapse | 4 cells |
| `aplysia_gill` | habituation, recovery, sensitisation | both of the above on one synapse | 9 cells |

Diagnosis: `dx_worm` (AVA > MA cut), `dx_leech` (HN_L > HN_R cut), `dx_aplysia` (TSN > FN cut); patient circuits are the references with the answer's weights at 0.
